The problem

Why Good Videos Get Ignored

Most videos don't fail on content — they fail on the click, or in the first ten seconds. Here's the pattern I see, and how I fix it.

The Creator's Problem

  • ✕ Thumbnails are an afterthought — made in five minutes after filming.
  • ✕ Great videos get skipped because the title and image don't create curiosity.
  • ✕ Editing eats the whole week, so uploads slip and the channel stalls.
  • ✕ Shorts lose viewers in two seconds — no hook, no captions, no pacing.
  • ✕ The final result never looks as professional as the channels you admire.

Working With Me

  • ✓ Thumbnails designed first, around the click — tested composition, ≤3-word type.
  • ✓ Every design creates an open loop the video itself has to close.
  • ✓ Done-for-you design and editing, delivered on schedule — you just film.
  • ✓ Shorts hooked from frame one, with styled captions and sound design included.
  • ✓ Clean, consistent, on-brand visuals on every single upload.
My process

Your Content's Framework

A simple process designed to turn your ideas into designs and edits that actually move people.

01

Brief & Concept

Before any pixel moves, we lock the direction — your video's promise, your audience, and the single emotion the thumbnail or edit has to trigger.

02

Design & Edit

Every piece is built around contrast, timing and pacing — bold type, clean composition, captions and sound shaped for retention from the first second.

03

Review & Deliver

You get a preview first, revisions until it feels right, then final files delivered on time and ready to upload — 1280×720 for thumbnails, 1080×1920 for Shorts.
